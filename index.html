FIELD GUIDE

From domain to evidence

  1. Enter an authorized domain and select Prepare research.
  2. Research links fill automatically. Certificate loading runs only when selected.
  3. Use live DNS only when you accept its visibility limits.
  4. Review the DNS results and use Copy Report for your notes.
A NOTE ON VISIBILITY

Public doesn’t mean anonymous.

ReconScope sends no target notification. Providers may log searches, and live DNS may reach authoritative servers. Activity after opening an external link is outside this app’s control.
